# 🛒 Olist E-Commerce Data Analysis | PostgreSQL & Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
E-commerce data analysis using **PostgreSQL and Power BI** to explore payment patterns, delivery performance, customer behaviour, and customer satisfaction.

📂 **Dataset:** Olist E-Commerce Dataset (9 CSV files), provided through the ExcelR Bootcamp program.

🎯 **Project Brief:** Five real-world business questions assigned as part of the ExcelR Project Bootcamp.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- 🐘 **PostgreSQL:** Data loading, validation, SQL queries, joins, aggregations, and business analysis.
- 📊 **Power BI:** Interactive dashboards, KPI visualisation, and business insights.

## 🧹 Data Checks & Validation
- 🔢 Checked row counts across seven tables.
- 📊 Analysed order status distribution and percentages.
- 🔗 Identified multiple reviews and payment types per order to prevent join fan-out and inflated counts.
- ⚠️ Checked for missing delivery dates.

## 📈 Business Questions & Analysis
1. 📅 Weekday vs Weekend Payment Statistics.
2. ⭐ Number of 5-Star Orders Paid by Credit Card.
3. 🚚 Average Delivery Days for Pet Shop Orders.
4. 💰 Average Price and Payment Values for São Paulo Customers.
5. 📦 Relationship Between Shipping Days and Review Scores.

## 💡 Key Findings
- 📅 **Order Distribution:** Weekdays accounted for 77% of orders, while weekends accounted for 23%.
- ⭐ **Customer Reviews & Payments:** Identified 43,981 orders with 5-star reviews and credit card payments.
- 🚚 **Delivery Performance:** Late orders received an average review score of 2.57, compared with 4.29 for on-time orders.
